Frequently Asked Questions

What happens if I don't fix this? ▼

This is not something you can ignore. If the issue isn't fixed, it can lead to several serious problems:

  • Your store may disappear from Google — search engines may not index your pages properly, or worse, they could index your private data.
  • You'll lose organic traffic and customers — every day this stays broken, potential customers can't find you.
  • Your SEO rankings will crash — once Google flags your store as misconfigured, recovery takes months.
  • Hackers can find your admin panel — the misconfiguration exposes paths like /admin, /orders, and /account to anyone who looks.
  • Customer data could be scraped — order history, account details, and checkout information are accessible to automated bots.
  • Your store could be hijacked — attackers can use this information to launch targeted attacks on your login system.
  • Other technical problems pile up — the longer you wait, the harder it becomes to diagnose and fix everything.
Is it fixable? ▼

Yes, it's fixable. However, it requires technical knowledge and coding experience. If you're not familiar with coding, it's best to have a professional handle it.

To fix the issue, the developer will need the SG file along with access to the relevant configuration files to identify the source of the misconfiguration and apply the necessary corrections without affecting your store's functionality.

How did this happen? ▼

It can happen for several reasons:

  • Theme code changes — an update or customization accidentally modifies the robots.txt configuration.
  • Too many apps — installing too many apps can create conflicts. Some apps automatically edit SEO or indexing settings and may overwrite one another's changes.
  • Incomplete app removal — when an app is removed, it may leave behind broken configuration.
  • Third-party code — external scripts or integrations can inject incorrect settings.
  • Manual editing errors — an incorrect manual edit to theme files can result in a misconfigured robots.txt file.
